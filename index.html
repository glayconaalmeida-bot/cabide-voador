Cabide Voador Studio

The fashion-video studio behind the TikTok account @cabidevoador

Cabide Voador Studio is a personal desktop tool (Windows) used by the creator of the TikTok account @cabidevoador. It edits short fashion videos on the creator's own computer and sends the finished files to the creator's TikTok drafts (inbox). The creator then opens TikTok, reviews the video, writes the caption, turns on the AI-generated content label and publishes it personally.

The app never publishes anything by itself and is used only with the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. Log in with TikTok (Login Kit): the creator authorizes the app once in the browser.
  2. Send to drafts (Content Posting API, video.upload): the creator picks a finished video and the app uploads it to the TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. Finish in TikTok: the creator taps the inbox notification, edits, labels AI content and posts.
  4. Performance report (Display API, user.info.stats and video.list): the app reads the views, likes, comments and shares of the creator's own videos to plan the next videos.

O Cabide Voador Studio é uma ferramenta pessoal de computador usada pelo criador da conta @cabidevoador no TikTok. Ela edita vídeos curtos de moda no próprio computador do criador e envia o arquivo pronto para os rascunhos (caixa de entrada) do TikTok. O criador abre o TikTok, revisa, escreve a legenda, liga o rótulo Conteúdo gerado por IA e publica pessoalmente. O app nunca publica sozinho.
